Conference History

COCOON has been held annually since 1995. COCOON 2027 in Melbourne is the 33rd edition of the conference and the third held in Australia, after Sydney in 2000 and 2012.

More information about the conference series is available at cocoon-conference.org. Proceedings of previous editions are listed on SpringerLink and dblp.
